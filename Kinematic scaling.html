Start with unit squares: 1 s wide and 1 m/s high.
Step 1 — Time: Move the slider. The origin and axes stay fixed while each bar narrows to Δt/n.

Step 2 — Speed: Click the green button. The speeds scale by 1/n, turning the thin cells back into perfect squares.

Therefore, the distance represented by one small square scales as 1/n².
